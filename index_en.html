Research

In the Biochemical Physics Laboratory, we investigate the structure, function, and interactions of biomacromolecules such as proteins using physicochemical approaches, mainly NMR spectroscopy and small-angle X-ray scattering (SAXS). Our goal is to understand biological phenomena and contribute to applications such as drug discovery.


Main Research Topics:

  • Protein Structure Analysis
  • Dynamics of Biomacromolecules
  • Development of Measurement and Analysis Methods for NMR Spectroscopy
  • Computational Chemistry Simulations
